## Decision rules

- A failure attributed to a weak comparator remains comparator uncertainty until
  BeatThis evidence, object placement, and at least one independent source are
  examined.
- Parameter changes require a frozen baseline, one Experiment Card, and a
  result entry before the next change.
- A v3 result may fall back to v2, but fallback count and failure reason are
  headline metrics.
- No algorithm is accepted from aggregate improvement alone; duration, tempo,
  label-confidence, and change-family strata must be checked.
