## Question and decision boundary

weakened independent-LN bodies and uncontrolled actual star ranges. Before
selecting another learner, distinguish a currently unavoidable short action

establishes one/two-onset forcing probability on three fixed old histories;

no trained model, generation, support, objective or previous judgment.

## Live branches and limits

An excess of forced states supports investigating earlier occupancy/release
planning or the distribution of generated histories. An excess of avoidable
choices supports investigating the immediate scorer/objective or decoding.
Mixed results require both mechanisms; neither result identifies a unique
neural cause. Source history has different occupancy and choices, so comparison
with it is descriptive rather than a counterfactual policy oracle.

Explicit requested difficulty/structure is a separate information branch.
[FIGARO](https://arxiv.org/html/2201.10936v4) learns description-to-sequence
conditioning from extracted musical attributes; that mechanism could motivate
an explicitly declared chart-profile condition. Its MIDI features and quality
results do not validate mania LN coordination or Pulsefield demand semantics.
The current audit introduces no such condition and makes no novelty claim.
The V3 formulation permits optional controls but does not define star rating or
LN amount as its final demand/style representation.
